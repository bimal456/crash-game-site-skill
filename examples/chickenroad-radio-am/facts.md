## The scam / clone problem (true and important)
- Search results and app stores are full of apps called "Chicken Road" or "Chicken Cross" made by unrelated developers (e.g. Google Play listings from various studios, Softonic downloads). These are not InOut's game. InOut does not publish a standalone real-money app; real-money play happens inside a licensed operator's site or that operator's own app.
- Fake "predictor", "signal" and "hack" bots/Telegram channels and inflated TikTok/YouTube "win" videos circulate. Outcomes are random (server-side RNG); no predictor works.
- Lookalike games from other studios exist: Mission Uncrossable (Roobet), Chicken Route (Turbo Games), Chicken Cross apps. Tell readers to verify "InOut Games" in the game's info panel.
